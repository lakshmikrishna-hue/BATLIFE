Why Maintain 20% – 80% Battery Charge?

Extended Lifespan

Lithium-ion batteries experience the least chemical degradation when kept between 20% and 80% capacity.

Reduced Heat & Stress

Charging above 80% increases internal resistance and voltage stress, generating excessive wear heat.

Prevent Deep Discharge

Draining below 20% puts extreme voltage strain on battery electrodes, decreasing overall charge cycle counts.

Pro Tip: You can keep this web tab open or minimized while working. When your device reaches 80% while charging or drops to 20% while on battery, Charge Sentinel will ring an alarm and send a system notification!
